# MD//WORKS Privacy Policy

[日本語](privacy-policy-ja.md) | English

Last updated: October 19, 2026  
Provider: HKJPN  
Scope: MD//WORKS web application, installed PWA, and Microsoft Store version

## 1. Our approach

MD//WORKS (the “App”) is a Markdown editor designed primarily to edit and process documents on your device.

This policy explains what information the App handles, why it handles that information, how it stores it, when it connects to external services, and how you can manage your information.

## 2. Documents and files

The App processes text, filenames, images, and other document information that you enter, select, paste, or import. This processing takes place on your device to provide editing, preview, conversion, saving, and export features. Documents may contain personal information that you choose to include.

The App does not automatically upload document contents, filenames, work history, or storage passphrases to a server operated by the provider. The provider does not offer cloud storage or synchronization of your documents through the App.

File access and clipboard use are associated with your actions, such as opening, saving, copying, or pasting, and are subject to operating system and browser permission controls.

## 3. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information in local browser or application storage:

- Documents in progress, document names, and autosaved drafts;
- Document work history;
- Preferences, such as the theme and display settings; and
- Update check timestamps and retrieved version information.

The App uses storage mechanisms such as Local Storage and IndexedDB. It applies encryption to autosaved documents and work history. The optional Private Storage feature adds protection based on a passphrase you provide. Not all locally stored information, such as display preferences, is encrypted.

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it or the browser, operating system, or application environment clears the relevant storage. Local storage does not guarantee that your information will be backed up or remain available indefinitely.

If you save or export a file into a folder synchronized by OneDrive or another service, that synchronization is governed by your operating system and the settings and policies of the service you use.

## 4. Deep Edit Mode

If you enable Deep Edit Mode, the App records information about your editing activity in device memory. This may include editing timestamps, operation types, changes in character counts, filenames, and excerpts of inserted or deleted text.

The App does not automatically transmit this log externally. The in-memory log is cleared when the page reloads, and you can also delete it using the log-clearing feature.

If you copy, save, or paste the log into another application or service, that copy may remain separately. Logs may contain personal information or text that you have deleted from the document. Review them before sharing.

## 5. Connections to external services

The App connects to external services to deliver application files, check for updates, and retrieve resources needed for some features.

| Service | Main purpose |
| --- | --- |
| GitHub Pages | Delivering the App and related files |
| GitHub API | Checking the latest release |
| GitHub | Opening documentation, the repository, or support pages at your request |
| jsDelivr | Retrieving selected libraries, dictionaries, and resources for features such as mathematical notation and diagrams |
| Google Fonts | Retrieving fonts used for display |

These connections may disclose technical connection information to the destination service, such as your IP address, request time, requested URL, and browser or operating system information. The App does not attach document contents or work history as data sent with these requests.

Each service handles the information it receives according to its own policies. See:

- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- [jsDelivr Privacy Policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)

Information handled independently by Microsoft Store, Windows, or Microsoft Edge is also subject to the relevant Microsoft policies and your settings.

## 6. AI services and external links

The App does not automatically send your documents or editing logs to AI services.

If you use a browser AI sidebar, extension, external AI service, or another tool to share text or logs, that information is processed according to the settings, terms, and privacy policy of the service you choose.

When you follow an external link, the destination website handles information according to its own privacy policy.

## 7. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

The App does not include its own advertising system, advertising trackers, or behavioral analytics that send document contents or editing activity to the provider.

The provider does not sell document contents or work history or disclose them to third parties for advertising purposes.

## 8. Access, deletion, and security

You can view, edit, save, and delete your documents and history using the App.

- Use features such as “Clear Local Saved Data” to delete locally autosaved documents and work history.
- Use the relevant browser or application environment's site-data controls to remove stored information, including preferences.
- Delete files you have saved or exported separately at their saved locations.
- Clearing local saved data does not automatically remove a document currently displayed in the editor or copies saved elsewhere.

The provider cannot retrieve, restore, or delete documents or history stored only on your device.

The App uses measures such as local processing, encryption of saved document data, and integrity checks for selected external resources to help protect information. These measures do not guarantee protection against misuse of your device or disclosure of information you share with external services.

## 9. Contact and support

For questions about the App or this policy, contact the project through:

[MD//WORKS GitHub Issues](https://github.com/HKJPN/markdown-editor/issues)

Information you submit, such as your GitHub username, message, and attachments, is handled to respond to your request, investigate problems, and retain records needed for those purposes.

GitHub Issues are public. Do not post personal information, confidential documents, passwords, or other sensitive information. GitHub's features and policies also govern the retention and deletion of posts.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the App's features or information-handling practices change. The revised policy and its update date will be published on this page. Material changes will also be communicated in the App or on its distribution page where appropriate.
